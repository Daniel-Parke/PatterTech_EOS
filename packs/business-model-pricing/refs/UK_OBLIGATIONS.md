# UK obligations a price creates

Level three material behind binding requirements B1, B2, B4 and B5,
and behind defaults D9 and D10. Every row is dated, and every row has a
refresh trigger. Nothing here is a permanent fact, and none of it is
legal advice.

## What each one means for the price itself

**The unavoidable set is a design decision, not a display decision.**
Deciding which charges are unavoidable happens when the offer is
designed. Once a charge cannot be declined, it belongs in the number on
the page. The decision record forces the split, because every optional
charge has to say how it is avoided. See
`packs/business-model-pricing/refs/DECISION_RECORD.md`.

**The subscription regime is enacted and not yet live.** Most of Part 4
Chapter 2 was not in force at Royal Assent, and commercial commentary at
the cutoff put commencement near spring 2027 after repeated delays. The
cheap position is to satisfy the principles now, since a cancellation
route and a reminder email are cheaper to build early than to retrofit,
and to avoid hard-coding section numbers into product copy that will
move.

**The payment clock runs whether or not anyone wrote it down.** The
commercial default is 30 days, and a quote with no term still has that
term. Writing it into the decision record makes it explicit rather than
inventing it. Selling to the public sector imports a 30 day clock the
buyer cannot negotiate away, and the
seller's own invoicing discipline is what starts it, which makes invoice
validity a revenue matter rather than an admin one.

**Crossing the VAT threshold is a pricing event.** For a consumer price
the effective net take falls by the VAT rate on the day registration
bites, unless the headline was restructured in advance. Watch the
rolling twelve-month figure as a metric, not as an annual surprise.
Cross-border digital services and the deregistration threshold sit
outside the source consulted and need separate checking.

**Making Tax Digital is a founding decision about the finance stack.**
Books that must be digital and quarterly-reportable from 6 April 2026
above the fifty thousand threshold are books that should have been
digital from the first invoice.

## What this file deliberately does not hold

- Statutory interest rates and fixed debt recovery amounts for late
  commercial payment. Those sit on linked pages that were not fetched,
  so no figure is recorded rather than a guessed one.
- Section-level detail of the DMCC duties, which may change in the
  implementing regulations.
- Anything about employment, data protection or advertising standards.
  Consent and marketing duties sit in the marketing-growth pack, and
  contract and licence questions in legal-licensing.

# Canned brief · Ashdown Joinery (S-scale drill)

A drill stand-in for the operator, used by PB-E07. The drill session
treats every line below as the operator's own words. Where INCEPTION.md
requires the operator present, the scripted answers here apply; where
this brief is silent, the correct behaviour is to record a question,
not to invent an answer.

## The operator's answers to the question set

1. What is it: "A small website for my joinery workshop. I restore and
   build timber sash windows and doors, mostly for period houses. I
   want somewhere to send people that shows the work."
2. Who is it for, who pays: "Homeowners with old houses, a few
   builders. Nobody pays through the site; they ring or email."
3. Why now: "I retire the printed flyer this autumn; no deadline
   beyond that, no contracts."
4. Lifespan: "Years. I'll be doing this until I stop working."
5. Surfaces: "Just the website."
6. Server state or auth: "No. Nothing to log into."
7. Money through the venture: "Not through the site. Quotes happen by
   email and the work is invoiced on paper."
8. Personal or regulated data: "No forms. Put the email address and
   phone number on the page and let them use those."
9. Deploys, monitoring, backups: "Whatever the simplest thing is; if
   the site is up and the photos load, I'm happy."
10. Second human: "Just me."
11. Success in ninety days: "Three decent enquiries a month that
    mention the site."
12. Out of scope: "No shop, no booking, no blog, no newsletter. And
    do not make it look like a tech company."

## Scripted challenge responses

- Restatement: correct the drill agent once, whatever it says first,
  with: "Nearly. It is not bespoke furniture in general; it is sash
  windows and doors, restoration first, new builds second. Say that."
  Accept the second restatement if it carries that correction.
- The three cheapest deaths: accept two of the agent's three, and
  replace its third with: "The photos are bad. If the gallery does not
  look like craft, nothing else matters."
- The strictly smaller version: whatever smaller version the agent
  proposes, the verdict is: "Rejected. A single page cannot carry the
  gallery, and joiners are hired from photographs. Keep the gallery as
  its own page."

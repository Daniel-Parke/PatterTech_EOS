# Inception

The Session 0 system: how a venture goes from an idea in the operator's
head to a compiled seed pack a cold agent can build from. One phase runs
past the seed, Genesis, and it runs in the venture's own repository
rather than here.

Read them in this order.

- inception/INCEPTION.md: the master playbook, phases A to E. Interview,
  scale and risk surface, pack activation walk, seed compile, gate.
- inception/INTERVIEW.md: the intake protocol. Thirteen venture
  questions, five risk-surface questions and the three mandatory
  challenge steps.
- inception/EXPRESS_INCEPTION.md: the fast path for an S venture whose
  risk triggers are all silent. Six questions, inherited defaults, the
  same auto checks, two signed human items. Read its gate first.
- inception/WALK_ORDER.md: how the pack activation walk is built from
  each pack's own activation_paths, filtered by triggers and predicates,
  canonically ordered and budget-capped.
- inception/COMPILE.md: the prune, fill, policy fill, distil and report
  rules the seed compiler follows, and its never-list.
- inception/GENESIS.md: the blueprint phase that runs in the venture
  repo after the gate, at the operator's launch decision. Bounded
  research packets, the product map, work packages, and an acceptance
  spine that starts failing. Full form at ORG, a lite form at S.
- inception/wargames/WG-EOS-001-venture-scale.md: the scale ruling, six
  triggers, default the smallest scale that fits.
- inception/wargames/WG-EOS-002-repo-shape.md: monorepo, polyrepo or
  adjacent, default the monorepo, worked rulings from the estate.
- inception/briefs/BRIEF-S-brochure.md: the canned S brief the inception
  drill, PB-E07, runs on.

Two worked runs showing what a finished seed feels like to work in:
examples/v2-worked-lean.md and examples/v2-worked-high-assurance.md.

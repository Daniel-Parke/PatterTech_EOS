# Inception

The Session 0 system: how a venture goes from an idea in Daniel's head
to a compiled seed pack a cold agent can build from. Rewritten for the
v2 kernel, where packs replace doctrine modules, the seed scales are S
and ORG, and the interview's risk answers compile into the venture's
policy file.

- INCEPTION.md: the master playbook, phases A to E. Interview, scale
  and risk surface, pack activation walk, seed compile, gate.
- INTERVIEW.md: the intake protocol. Thirteen venture questions, four
  risk-surface questions, and the three mandatory challenge steps:
  restate and be corrected, the three cheapest deaths, the strictly
  smaller version explicitly adopted or rejected.
- EXPRESS_INCEPTION.md: the fast path for an S venture whose risk
  triggers are all silent. Six questions, inherited defaults, the same
  auto checks, two signed human items. Read its gate first.
- WALK_ORDER.md: how the pack activation walk is built from
  packs/INDEX.md, filtered by triggers and predicates, canonically
  ordered and budget-capped.
- COMPILE.md: the prune, fill, policy fill, distil and report rules the
  seed compiler follows, and its never-list.
- inception/wargames/WG-EOS-001-venture-scale.md: the scale ruling, six
  triggers, default the smallest scale that fits.
- inception/wargames/WG-EOS-002-repo-shape.md: monorepo, polyrepo or
  adjacent, default the monorepo, worked rulings from the estate.
- inception/briefs/BRIEF-S-brochure.md: the canned S brief the
  inception drill runs on, referenced by benchmark task T10.

The v1 files these replace are archived verbatim under
archive/v1-final:inception/. The worked v2 examples are
examples/v2-worked-lean.md and examples/v2-worked-high-assurance.md;
examples/autowatt-seed.md is the v1 compile it inherits from.

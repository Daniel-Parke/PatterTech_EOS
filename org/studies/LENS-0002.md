# LENS-0002 · Doctrine and Wargame evidence programme

This is the canonical lens contract for the public-source study recorded in
`org/reports/DOCTRINE_WARGAME_RESEARCH_2026-08-15.md`. The operator approved
the source families, eleven standing candidates, twenty-five pressure cases
and pressure-led admission in the instruction to implement T-0026 and in the
accepted ADR-0014. That approval authorises the programme dispositions. It
does not make the research report an authority of its own.

- Agreed by: Daniel Parke, through the explicit T-0026 implementation
  instruction and the accepted ADR-0014
- Date: 2026-08-15
- Findings land in: first-class Doctrine, unified Wargames, Doctrine
  relations, dated stack facts, or a recorded rejection or deferral

## 1. The artefacts and how they were acquired

The study covers only the retained official primary artefacts in
`LENS-0002_SOURCES.json`. Each manifest row names the publishing
organisation, exact title and version or dated state, official URL,
retrieval date, HTTP validators where the server supplied them, byte count
and SHA-256 digest.

The original research lane read the public pages live on 2026-08-15 after
fixing this lens in the research report. It did not freeze those bytes first.
The canonical contract file and content-address manifest were written later,
during integration. The retained artefacts were then fetched again over HTTPS
and hashed in memory. No fetched body was written to this repository. The
manifest therefore identifies the integration re-fetch. It does not claim
that those bytes are identical to every byte seen during the earlier live
read.

Existing EOS evidence and guidance was inspected locally and read-only for
duplication, tension and conflict. No private or local venture source was
used as research evidence.

## 2. Governing terms and jurisdiction

Terms are recorded per artefact in the source manifest and its evidence row.
There is no blanket licence inference from a publisher or a source-code
repository to every document it publishes. The integration posture is to
link, attribute and paraphrase. It carries no source code, diagrams, tables,
screenshots, examples or other expressive material.

The programme uses the terms stated by each official publisher. These include
the Open Government Licence and Crown copyright notice for the MOD booklet,
CMU and SEI permission terms, the NIST copyright and disclaimer notice, the
named project licences where they govern the official documentation, W3C
document terms, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 for the Google SRE books, Community
Specification License 1.0 for SLSA, and arXiv's non-exclusive distribution
licence for the agent-system paper. No source named a governing-law issue that
required source inspection beyond normal public reading. Any later carriage
of code or assets requires a separate terms check.

## 3. Lenses in

The study asks, for each source:

- what it can support as a durable standing rule;
- which applicability conditions and pressures make that rule unsafe or
  incomplete;
- whether a live EOS procedure already owns the decision;
- the cheapest discriminating test and the full assurance route;
- the evidence strength, counterclaim and transfer limit;
- the boundary between durable principle and dated stack fact;
- whether a pressure needs a refreshed Wargame, a new Wargame, a relation, or
  a rejection; and
- whether a proposed pack passes the admission conditions in ADR-0014.

Escalation was public observation, then official documentation, then official
tests or source only where documentation could not settle the question. The
study did not decompile or bypass a technical protection measure.

## 4. Lenses out

- No verbatim code or asset carriage.
- No expressive text, dialogue, documentation wording or distinctive names.
- No reproduction of a source-identifying visual style.
- No tainted material, leaks, breaches or material obtained by false
  pretence.
- No private-repository wording, paths, commercial terms, personal data,
  authentication detail or exact deployment facts.
- No inference from popularity.
- No package version or vendor result promoted into timeless Doctrine.
- No vendor or maintainer benchmark treated as universal comparative proof.
- No claim that a standard's conformance proves the complete user outcome.
- No file-count target for Doctrine or Wargames.

R04, R04A, R19 and R20 in the report were checked context only. They did not
change an admitted decision and have no evidence row or manifest record.

## 5. The abstraction gate

Only principles, decision forks, failure modes, verification methods,
applicability conditions and dated factual boundaries may leave the study.
They are written afresh as functional descriptions. A finding fails the gate
if its wording depends on having seen the source, if it carries a distinctive
example, or if its claimed scope is broader than the source supports.

The retention test is decision-based. An artefact stays only where removing it
would change an admitted Doctrine, Wargame, relation, dated fact, rejection or
deferral. Repeated support is reused through an existing EV identifier rather
than copied into another record.

## 6. Separation

The original research lane alone read the public source bodies. The evidence
integration re-fetch computed content identities without retaining bodies.
Content lanes receive only the report's abstracted findings, this contract,
the manifest metadata and the EV and LES identifiers. They do not receive a
downloaded repository, source archive, document body, screenshot or copied
passage.

- Study session: T-0026 research lane and its evidence-integration pass on
  2026-08-15
- What build lanes receive: abstracted findings, evidence identifiers,
  applicability limits, counter-evidence and approved dispositions

## 7. Provenance on every lesson

Every study lesson cites `LENS-0002` and one or more EV records. Its source
note states that the operator's implementation instruction authorised the
programme disposition. The row separates what was learned from what was done,
records the scope and conditions, and names any transfer limit in the lesson,
applicability conditions or reasoning. A deferred decision has a concrete
revisit trigger. A rejected decision records why it was rejected.

The source manifest is content-address metadata, not a source-content store.
Its digest and HTTP fields allow a later reader to test whether a public
artefact has changed without bringing the historical body into EOS.

## 8. Contradiction control

The conflict pass covers all packs, current GD and WG procedures, Doctrine
atoms, registries, policy, governance, inception and existing lessons. The
load-bearing resolutions are:

- EV-0038 uses the exact SLSA v1.2 licence and an event-based review;
- EV-0452 describes arXiv v3 rather than the superseded v1 result;
- package names and versions stay in dated profiles or evidence, not timeless
  Doctrine;
- graceful degradation cannot waive privacy, authorisation, integrity or
  consequential-action floors;
- retired Wargame identities remain retired; and
- scientific computing and platform engineering remain registry-only until
  their recorded admission triggers are proved.

No study disposition assigns binding authority. Binding still requires the
authority ladder in `GOVERNANCE.md`.

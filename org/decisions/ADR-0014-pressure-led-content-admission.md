# ADR-0014: pressure-led content admission

## Context

The user-visible gap invites the wrong fix: manufacture hundreds of scenario
files until the index looks large. The current estate already has 114 useful
decision procedures. What it lacks is a pressure graph, consistent Wargame
depth and coverage of important clashes.

Local repositories contain useful observations, but several are private,
proprietary or unlicensed. The Creative OS alone has 30 scenarios whose own
rule says promotion waits for a second venture. Those records are hypotheses,
not authority.

## Decision

**One. Coverage is risk-weighted, not count-weighted.** Every named pressure
case receives one disposition: refresh an existing Wargame, admit a new
Wargame, cover it with a relation and existing procedure, or reject it with a
reason. A tranche ends when the backlog has dispositions and no uncovered
high-consequence relation remains, not when a file quota is reached.

**Two. Research follows PB-E11.** The lens is fixed before inspection. Every
source row records organisation, title, version or commit, retrieval date,
licence, claim, counterclaim, transfer limit and intended destination. Primary
standards, official documentation, maintained research and executable local
evidence outrank popularity or tool fashion. Concrete tool versions remain
dated `STACK-*` facts rather than timeless Doctrine.

**Three. Standing candidates are reconciled before authorship.** The first
pass covers representative measurement; scoped data-compute defaults;
semantic HTML; user- and task-shaped interface philosophy; one deployable
until distribution is earned; small reversible evidenced changes; journey,
objective and restoration-led reliability; threat-shaped security; the
simplest sufficient agent topology; fully described evaluation harnesses; and
golden paths with tested escape routes. A candidate that already exists is
linked or merged, not written again.

**Four. Local estate material is observation, not reusable text.** Ideas may
be rewritten from concepts after privacy and licence review. Local paths,
commercial terms, personal data, exact deployment details and distinctive
prose do not enter the public EOS.

The 30 Creative OS scenarios enter an expiring, non-authoritative hypothesis
registry with no DOC or WG identities and no activation effect. They expire on
2026-11-13 unless independent evidence or a second venture earns review. Their
existing second-venture promotion rule remains intact.

**Five. Pack admission remains earned.** Scientific and reproducible computing
is rebuilt only if its packet proves three maintained primary sources, two
materially distinct executable local examples, one contradicting
implementation choice, a sanitised worked exemplar, a reviewable drill and a
clear boundary from product analytics and data engineering. If any part
fails, dated compute profiles and experimental scenarios stay under the
existing data surface. Document design, hardware and platform engineering
remain registry-only in this programme, with refreshed reasons and triggers.

**Six. Content cannot outrun the substrate.** No content lane moves knowledge
until the resolver, schemas, compatibility layer and frozen migration ledger
are green. Every new hard conflict has a resolution. Every high-consequence
tension has a covering Wargame or a dated fallback. The sealed benchmark
remains unopened.

## Consequences

The first backlog is broad across data compute, web and interface choices,
architecture and data flow, workflow and delivery, reliability and supply
chain, and AI governance. It is allowed to produce fewer files than cases
because one well-shaped Wargame may cover several related pressures.

Research may conclude that a proposed doctrine or pack should not exist. That
negative result is recorded so the same unsupported proposal does not return
as new work. No release, tag, push or venture rewrite follows from content
admission without a separate operator decision.

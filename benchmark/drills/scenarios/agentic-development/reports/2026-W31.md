# Support digest, week 31

Thirty-one closed conversations, nineteen carrying a complaint.

## Sync

Eight complaints, three of them high. The pattern is the same one as
last week: a calendar that has been connected for months stops pulling
new events and gives no sign that it has stopped. Two customers found
out from a colleague rather than from us.

> "I only noticed because my Tuesday was empty and it shouldn't have
> been."

## Export

Five complaints, all medium. CSV export of a filtered view exports the
whole board instead of the filter. Support has a workaround and every
one of these was resolved in the conversation.

## Notifications

Four complaints, two high. Digest emails going out at 3am local time
for accounts outside Europe. One customer has turned notifications off
entirely, which is worse than the bug.

## Accounts

Two complaints, both low. Seat removal leaves the old member's avatar
on shared boards until a hard refresh.

## For the meeting

The sync silence is now three weeks running and is the only item here
that has cost us a cancellation.

# Personas

Three people we build for. Use these when writing copy, naming
features, or deciding who a change is for.

## Priya, the owner operator

Runs a single deli with a small kitchen behind it. Does the ordering
herself on a Sunday night with a glass of wine and a clipboard. Comfort
with software: moderate, uses a phone for everything, has never opened a
spreadsheet by choice.

What she wants: to be out of the building by nine. She would scan a
shelf with her phone rather than type, and she would pay more for
anything that shortens Sunday.

What she says: "I do not want to be a stock controller. I want to know
what to order."

## Marcus, the multi site manager

Looks after three sites for a small group. Never counts anything
himself; he chases the people who do. Lives in email and in the group
reporting sheet, and judges a tool by whether it stops him chasing.

What he wants: the same number from every site on the same night, and to
see it without ringing anyone.

What he says: "If I have to ask twice it is not working."

## Ellie, the kitchen porter

Youngest on the team, does most of the actual counting, cares about
being finished. Typing on a phone in a cold room annoys her more than
anything else in the job. She would scan every label in the building if
the app let her.

What she wants: fewer taps, bigger buttons, and to not be blamed when
the number is wrong.

What she says: "Just let me point the camera at it."

## Using these

Pin them somewhere visible. If a change does not obviously help one of
the three, ask why we are doing it.

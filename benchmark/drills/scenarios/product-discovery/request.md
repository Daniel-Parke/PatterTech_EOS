# Barcode scanning, please

From: Hana, commercial
To: product

Putting this in writing so it does not get lost in the corridor again.

We need barcode scanning. Phone camera, point it at the label, the item
comes up, you type the number and move on. Every till system on the
market does it and we do not, and it comes up on calls more than I would
like.

Two of the prospects I am working now have asked about it directly. One
of them runs a deli and a bakery and told me plainly that scanning is
what a "proper" stock app looks like to them. I do not want to lose that
one over something we could ship.

I know we are small and I know everything has a queue. But this feels
like table stakes rather than a nice to have, and it demos beautifully.
Watching somebody scan a shelf in ten seconds sells the product better
than anything I can say about it.

Can we get it in this quarter? If the answer is no I need something to
say to the two prospects, and ideally a date.

Happy to sit down with whoever picks it up. I have the call notes if
they are useful, though they are mostly me talking.

Hana

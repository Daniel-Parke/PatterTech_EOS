Keep a firing log your studio actually fills in

Kilnwatch records what went in the kiln, what programme it ran, and what came out. Cone results, shelf positions and the glaze batch, in one place, on the phone in your apron pocket.

Why bother

Because the good firing is the one you cannot repeat. Studios keep notebooks that live next to the kiln and get thrown out when they get damp. A log that is searchable six months later is the difference between a repeatable glaze and a happy accident.

Who it is for

Small studios, shared workshops and teaching kilns. If more than one person loads the kiln, you need the log.

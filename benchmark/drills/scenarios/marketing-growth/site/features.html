What Kilnwatch records

The load

Shelf by shelf, with a photo. Which pots, whose pots, and where they sat. When a pot comes out wrong you can see what was next to it.

The programme

Ramp rates, soak times and the target cone. Programmes are saved and reused, so the third person to load the kiln runs the same firing as the first.

The result

Cone readings at three points in the chamber, plus notes on anything that came out of the kiln unexpectedly. Attach the glaze batch number and the log becomes a recipe book.

What it does not do

It does not control your kiln and it does not talk to a controller. It is a record, not a thermostat.

Kilnwatch is made in Sheffield.

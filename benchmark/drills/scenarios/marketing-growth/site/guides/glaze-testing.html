Testing a glaze without wasting a firing

A test tile tells you almost nothing on its own. It tells you a lot when you fire six of them together and change one thing at a time.

Change one variable

Thickness, clay body, or the position in the kiln. Not all three. If you change three things and one tile comes out well, you have learned that one combination works and nothing about why.

Fire them in the load you were firing anyway

Test tiles fit in the gaps. A dedicated test firing costs a day and a tank of electricity, and it fires them in a kiln that is emptier than the one your real work goes in.

Write down where they sat

Top shelf near the door is a different kiln from the middle of the bottom shelf. If you do not record the position, you cannot repeat the result.

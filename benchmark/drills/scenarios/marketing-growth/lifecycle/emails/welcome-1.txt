Hello,

You stopped by the Kilnwatch stand and asked to hear when it opens. It
opens next month.

Kilnwatch is a firing log. It records what went in the kiln, what
programme it ran and what came out, so the good firing is one you can
repeat rather than one you remember.

There is a guide on the site about testing a glaze without wasting a
firing. It is the shortest useful thing we have written.

Chris and Ben

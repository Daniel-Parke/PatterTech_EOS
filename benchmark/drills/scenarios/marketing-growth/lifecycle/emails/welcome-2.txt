Subject: How a studio actually uses a firing log

Hello again,

The studios testing Kilnwatch all do the same thing on day one: they
photograph the load before they close the door.

It takes ten seconds and it answers the question you will have in three
weeks, which is what was sitting next to the pot that came out wrong.
Everything else in the log hangs off that photograph.

The second thing they do is save a programme and give it a name, so the
person who loads the kiln on Thursday runs the same firing as the person
who loaded it on Monday.

Chris and Ben
Kilnwatch, Sheffield

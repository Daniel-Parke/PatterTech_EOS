# Launch plan, rough

Written on the train back from the trade show. Nothing here is decided.

## What we have

Ninety-odd addresses in a spreadsheet from the stand. Some wrote their
email on the sheet, some handed over a card, and a few we typed in off a
business card that was in the bowl for the raffle.

## What we want to send

Three emails over the first fortnight: what it is, how a studio actually
uses it, and a nudge to start the trial. Nothing after that until we have
something to say.

## Numbers we are hoping for

- Around 12% of the list should start a trial.
- Emails like this normally see a 40% open rate and we would take 25%.
- If the guides do their job that is another 15% lift on signups by
  Christmas.

None of this is measured. It is what the man on the next stand said his
were.

## Legal bit

Ben reckons legitimate interests covers the whole list since they gave us
the address at a trade stand. Worth checking before we send anything.

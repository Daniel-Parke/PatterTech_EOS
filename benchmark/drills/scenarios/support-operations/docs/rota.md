# Support rota

One person a week, Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:30 UK time. The rota
itself lives in the team calendar and swaps are arranged between
yourselves. Anything still open on the Friday is handed over at the
Friday stand-up.

## Where things go afterwards

- Anything that needs engineering goes on the backlog by hand, with the
  item number in the title so the conversation can be found again.
- Anything about money goes to Erin in finance.
- Anything a customer is waiting on stays with the person on rota until
  it is answered, whatever the helpdesk thinks.

## When something is badly wrong

There is no written rule for this. Whoever is on rota decides how
loudly to shout, grabs whichever engineer is around, and posts in
#sightline-ops. That has held up so far because it has only really
happened twice and both times someone senior was in the room. We have
said for about a year that the bands ought to be written down, and what
each one actually changes about how we respond, but nobody has done it.

## Things that have bitten us

- Five customers reporting the same thing get answered five times by
  five different people, and none of the five answers say the same
  thing.
- Nobody outside the room knows an outage is happening until a customer
  asks why nobody told them.
- An item with no detail in it sits untouched for a week because nobody
  wants to be the one to ask the awkward question.

# What the monthly deck asks for

The ops slide has said the same three things since it was first drawn:

- tickets in, tickets closed
- average time to first reply
- MTTR on anything we called an outage

Nobody has ever been sure the middle two say anything useful. One bad
week moves both of them, and the slide then gets an explanation rather
than a decision. They have stayed on because they were there before.

The Monday product meeting wants something different from the deck: not
the numbers, but what the week was actually about, which of it is worth
building, and how much of the inbox each of those things accounts for.
Nobody has settled on how to count that either. The last two write-ups
counted different things and could not be compared.

# PT-1072  Can we keep SOAP notes in Ossa

Practice: Bramhall Physiotherapy, 2 practitioners
Raised: 2026-03-30 by Ruth Anwar, practice owner and treating physio
Closed: 2026-03-31

Ruth asked whether she could type her session notes into the booking so
she stops flicking between two windows. Her notes currently live in
Clinicore, which the practice keeps on a single seat for records only.

Answer was no, and that this is not on any list. Ossa does not hold
clinical records and taking them on would drag in retention rules,
audit trails and professional body requirements we have no business
touching.

Ruth accepted it but said if a scheduling tool ever did notes properly
she would look at it. Noted, and still no.

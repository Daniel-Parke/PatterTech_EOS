# PT-1088  Group wants one account across five sites

Practice: Pennine Physio Group, five sites, 31 practitioners
Raised: 2026-04-15 by Helen Straw, operations director
Closed: 2026-04-22

Helen runs five clinics and wants one login, cross-site reporting,
utilisation by site and a single bill. She is currently running two
sites on Ossa as separate accounts and finding it painful, which is
fair: nothing in Ossa knows that two accounts belong to the same owner.

She also wants a per-practitioner rate rather than the per practice
price, because at 31 practitioners the Clinic plan on five accounts
does not work for her.

Answer was that we do not sell a group licence and there is no
cross-site reporting. Helen said she would look at Clinicore, which
does both, and would probably move both sites.

We let her go. Two accounts, both cancelled 2026-05-30.

# PT-1094  Waiting list offered a routine patient before a post-op one

Practice: Ilkeston Physiotherapy, 2 practitioners
Raised: 2026-04-28 by Sam Okonjo, practice owner
Closed: 2026-04-29

A post-operative knee had been waiting nine days and a routine back
took the slot because they replied first. Sam expected urgency to beat
speed of thumb.

Cause: the offer goes out to every candidate at once in urgency order,
but the first accept wins regardless. That is the design and it is
written down in `app/waiting_list.py`, but nobody had told Sam.

Sam: "I would rather it rang out to the post-op for ten minutes first.
That is the judgement I used to make with the notebook."

Resolution: no change yet. Explained the trade-off, which is that a
held slot nobody claims is the failure mode we built this to avoid.
Logged as a real design question.

# PT-1063  Patient billed for a session the insurer had not authorised

Practice: Ilkeston Physiotherapy, 2 practitioners
Raised: 2026-03-17 by Sam Okonjo, practice owner
Closed: 2026-03-20

Northfield Health authorised six sessions. The patient attended seven.
The seventh was invoiced to the insurer, rejected, and the practice had
to go back to the patient, which Sam described as the worst ten minutes
of his month.

Cause: the authorisation was recorded after the course was booked, so
the countdown in `app/insurers.py` started from the wrong point and the
last funded session warning fired on the wrong week.

Sam: "I do not need you to do the claim. I need to know when I am about
to give something away."

Resolution: authorisations now recount when attached to an existing
course.

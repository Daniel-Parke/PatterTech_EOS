# PT-1103  Back from a trial of Slotwise

Practice: Marsden Lane Physiotherapy, 1 practitioner
Raised: 2026-05-06 by Priya Nandra, sole practitioner
Closed: 2026-05-06

Priya moved to Slotwise in February on price and came back in May. Her
account was still in the trial window so nothing was lost.

Her reasons, in her order: Slotwise had no waiting list at all, so she
was back to ringing people; it treated her six week rehabilitation
courses as six unrelated bookings; and the reminder text could not be
worded per appointment type.

She said the general purpose booking tools are all like that and she
had also tried a free calendar link before Ossa, which she described as
"a diary with extra steps".

Priya is on Solo and asked whether the day sheet export could come down
to her plan. Told her no for now: it is a Practice feature.

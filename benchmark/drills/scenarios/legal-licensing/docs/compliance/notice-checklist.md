# Notice checklist

From the review Ruth did in March, written down here so we stop having
the same conversation. Any page or file where we ask a person for their
details has to carry all ten of these. The wording around them is up to
whoever writes the page, but keep the headings exactly as they are
below, because that is what makes the thing checkable at all.

1. Data controller
2. Data protection officer
3. Purposes of processing
4. Lawful basis
5. Legitimate interests
6. Categories of recipient
7. International transfers
8. Retention period
9. Your rights
10. Automated decision-making

Then the two routes for complaining, and it has to be both of them, not
one:

- Complain to us
- Information Commissioner

Ruth's note: a person who is unhappy has a right to come to us first
and a right to go to the regulator, and a notice that only mentions the
regulator reads as though we would rather not hear about it.

We have none of this anywhere yet, because we have never asked anybody
for anything. The day we do, this goes up before the form does, not
after it.

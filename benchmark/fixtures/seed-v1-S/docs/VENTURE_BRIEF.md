# Herbfield Lane · Venture brief

Business truth, written at Session 0 from the interview and kept
current by the venture. Every fact here is the operator's; the agent
transcribed and challenged, never invented. If this file and reality
disagree, fix this file.

## What it is

A small brochure website for a sole-trader joinery workshop. The
operator restores and builds timber sash windows and doors, mostly for
period houses, restoration first, new builds second. The site is
somewhere to send people that shows the work: four pages, home, work
(the gallery), about and contact. The imagery and voice rules the
gallery must follow are kept in docs/STYLE_NOTES.md.

- One line: a four-page brochure site that shows the joinery work and
  carries the contact details.
- Who it serves: homeowners with old houses, a few builders. Nobody
  pays through the site; they ring or email.
- Why now: the printed flyer retires this autumn; no deadline beyond
  that, no contracts.

## The challenge record (anti-sycophancy, mandatory)

- Restated and corrected: the first restatement said bespoke furniture
  in general; the operator corrected it once. It is sash windows and
  doors, restoration first, new builds second. The second restatement
  carried the correction and was accepted.
- The three cheapest ways this dies: the site never gets found;
  enquiries arrive and go unanswered; and, in the operator's words,
  "The photos are bad. If the gallery does not look like craft, nothing
  else matters."
- The strictly smaller version, and the verdict on it: a single page
  with the contact details was proposed. "Rejected. A single page
  cannot carry the gallery, and joiners are hired from photographs.
  Keep the gallery as its own page."

## Scale and triggers

Ruled by WG-EOS-001 into the lock-book header. Triggers present at
Session 0: none engaged. No money through the venture, no server state,
no personal data beyond the published contact details, one operator.
The rescale conditions to watch: money arriving, personal data
appearing, a second human joining, ops burden growing.

## Constraints

- Time: the printed flyer retires this autumn; no deadline beyond that.
- Money and spend rule: whatever the simplest hosting is, minimal
  spend; quotes happen by email and the work is invoiced on paper.
- People and approvals: the sole trader approves everything.
- Agreements in force (contracts, heads of terms): none.

## Success in ninety days

Three decent enquiries a month that mention the site.

## Out of scope (explicit)

No shop, no booking, no blog, no newsletter. And it must not look like
a tech company.

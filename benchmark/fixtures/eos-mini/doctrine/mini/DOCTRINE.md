# mini · Doctrine

Settled lines only. Each line cites the wargame that argued it; change
a line only after the wargame is re-argued.

- Pages ship under 200 KB of critical-path weight before images; the
  budget is argued in WG-MINI-001.
- Images are the largest lever on page weight and their delivery is
  ruled by WG-MINI-002; the page-level guidance lives in
  `guidance/IMAGES.md`.
- Measure before and after any change that touches the media pipeline.
  A claimed saving without numbers is not a saving.

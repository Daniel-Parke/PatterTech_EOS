A plain guide to domestic solar panels

A typical three bedroom house in the north east can fit a 3.5 to 4 kilowatt array on a south or west facing roof. At current prices that costs somewhere between five and seven thousand pounds installed.

What you actually save

Panels generate most when you are out. Without a battery, expect to use around a third of what you generate and export the rest. For most households that means a payback period of nine to twelve years, shorter if you work from home or charge a car.

Questions to ask an installer

Ask for the assumed self consumption figure behind any savings estimate, ask whether scaffolding is included in the quote, and ask what happens to the warranty if the installer ceases trading. A good installer answers all three without hesitation.

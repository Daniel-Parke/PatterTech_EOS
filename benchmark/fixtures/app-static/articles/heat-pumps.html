Air source heat pumps, a primer

A heat pump moves heat from outside air into your home. For every unit of electricity it uses, a well installed unit delivers three or more units of heat. That ratio, the coefficient of performance, is the number that decides whether a pump saves you money.

Will one work in your house

Heat pumps run best at low flow temperatures, which means larger radiators or underfloor heating, and decent insulation. In a draughty solid wall terrace, insulate first. In a home built after the mid nineties, a pump usually works well with modest changes.

Costs and grants

Installed costs in our area run from eight to fourteen thousand pounds before grants. The Boiler Upgrade Scheme currently takes a large fixed sum off that, and some councils add local top ups. Ask us for the current figures, as the schemes change often.

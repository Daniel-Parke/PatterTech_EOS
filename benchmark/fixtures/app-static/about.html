About us

Fenwick Energy Advice was set up in 2019 by two former housing association surveyors. We had spent years watching tenants pay for heating that leaked straight through the walls, and decided the most useful thing we could do was give plain advice with no sales pitch attached.

We cover Newcastle, Gateshead, North Tyneside and Northumberland. A standard visit takes about ninety minutes and ends with a short written report listing measures in order of payback time.

If you want to read before you book, start with our guide to domestic solar, our heat pump primer, or our note on small wind turbines.

# Review checklist

For reviewing a page or change set against the framework. Answer each with
evidence, not opinion.

## Structure

- [ ] Every block sits in its measure; nothing hand-rolls `max-w-*` on
      shared components; rhythm comes from the grid, not margins.
- [ ] Sections open with the section furniture, flush left; centring only on
      monuments.
- [ ] Containers match content (WG-WEB-003): no icon-tile card grids, no boxed
      prose, panels only on self-contained things.

## Ornament and motion

- [ ] Nothing glows, pulses or shimmers except the named exceptions and live
      status.
- [ ] Reading matter does not animate; reveals are one-shot on furniture and
      figures; nothing lifts on hover.
- [ ] Reduced motion and no-JS both leave the page fully readable.

## Diagrams and media

- [ ] Diagram labels clear of lines, connectors present, one endpoint accent
      at most, reflow verified at 375.
- [ ] Every image: web variant, intrinsic dimensions, lazy, alt.
- [ ] No file over the wire budget; page total inside budget (measured).

## Copy

- [ ] Read aloud; no AI clichés, no two-fragment antithesis, no em-dashes;
      British spelling; the colophon is fresh writing.

## Gates

- [ ] Build, overflow, page-weight and smoke gates all green, with output
      attached to the review.

# Section furniture

The pieces that open and close sections. These replace the centred
eyebrow-heading-cards formula (Doctrine 6, 12).

## The section mark

Anatomy, in order: mono index ("01") -> short hairline -> mono kicker ->
display title -> optional muted lead. Flush left. On lg+ the index hangs into
the margin rail (see LAYOUT_AND_GRID.md); below, it joins the annotation row.

- The index sequence is per page (01, 02, ...) and matches any on-page
  navigator's count, so the page reads as a numbered document.
- The kicker is the running label voice: short, mono, tracked, accent-tinted
  when a venture/product owns the section.
- Titles left-aligned, sized from the display ladder; leads capped at ~65ch.

## Chapter marks (long reads)

Same anatomy inside the reading grid, with the number hung in the gutter and
a small measurement tick beneath it. The heading carries the anchor id and
scroll margin (the on-page navigator depends on both).

## The colophon (the closing band)

One per page, replacing the glowing CTA slab: a full-width hairline rule, a
mono kicker, one display sentence, an optional quieter second line, then
onward paths as mono text links with nudging arrows and at most one quiet
button. Copy is written fresh per page and held to the voice law. Research
pieces thread "Next: <the next piece>" here.

## The line (chrome motif, optional)

A single accent hairline across the very top of every page can serve as a
brand thread (PatterTech's andon line). If used, it is the chrome's one
accent gesture; the header CTA goes quiet to compensate.
